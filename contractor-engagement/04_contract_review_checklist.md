# CONTRACT REVIEW CHECKLIST - CONTRACTOR / SME AGREEMENT
tags: #career #contracting #legal | rev: 2026-10-29

Use when they send a master services agreement (MSA), contractor agreement or vendor packet. No contract exists yet; this is the checklist to run it against. Positions mirror `01_call_prep_sheet.md` section 40.

Severity rubric: **STOP** = do not sign until changed · **NEGOTIATE** = push back, can concede with a fallback · **ACCEPT** = normal, just confirm.

## 30. BEFORE YOU SIGN

```
[ ] Every STOP item resolved in writing (redline or amendment)
[ ] Rate, block and payment terms match 02 output and the accepted proposal
[ ] SOW attached and referenced; deliverables and acceptance criteria present
[ ] Insurance quotes obtained for any required limits; cost priced into rate
[ ] W-9 sent with LLC name and EIN (not your SSN, if LLC)
[ ] Attorney review of IP, indemnity, liability cap, contractor status (first contract only)
[ ] Countersigned copy and PO number in hand before day 1
```

## HANDOFF

```
DECISIONS MADE   STOP items are deal-breakers until changed; NEGOTIATE items have fallbacks.
OPEN QUESTIONS   Your insurance carrier and limits; your state's non-compete law.
INPUTS NEEDED    Their contract or vendor packet; prior-agreement answers (01 section 30).
```
