# PROPOSAL + STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW) - TEMPLATE
tags: #career #contracting #sow | rev: 2026-10-29

Two parts. **Part A** is the 1-page proposal you send after call 1. **Part B** is the SOW that becomes the contract exhibit once they accept. Replace every `<<FIELD>>`. Delete this header block and the `[NOTE]` lines before sending. Work on a copy in `private/`.

Rates come from `02_rate_model_output.md`. Quote TARGET or OPEN; never below FLOOR.

---

## PART A - PROPOSAL

**To:** <<CLIENT CONTACT NAME, TITLE>>, <<CLIENT COMPANY>>
**From:** <<YOUR NAME>>, <<YOUR LLC NAME, if any>>
**Date:** <<DATE>> · **Valid until:** <<DATE + 30 d>>
**Re:** <<ENGAGEMENT TITLE>>

### 1. What I heard

<<Their problem in 2-3 sentences, in their words from the call. Name the cost of delay if they gave one.>>

### 2. Proposed approach

<<3-5 sentences: what you will do, in what order, and why your background shortens the path.>>

### 3. Deliverables

```
#   DELIVERABLE                         FORMAT              DUE
D1  <<e.g., assessment of X>>           <<report, n pp>>    <<date or week n>>
D2  <<e.g., recommendations / plan>>    <<briefing>>        <<date>>
D3  <<e.g., on-call SME support>>       <<hours/mo>>        <<ongoing>>
```

### 4. Fee options

```
OPTION                       PRICE                                   BEST IF
1  Monthly retainer          $<<TARGET x block x (1 - discount)>>/mo   you expect steady questions and
   (<<n>> h/mo block)        overage at $<<TARGET>>/h                  want guaranteed access
2  Time and materials        $<<TARGET>>/h, invoiced monthly         scope is still unclear
3  Fixed fee for D1-D2       $<<computed: est. hours x OPEN>>         scope is tight and well defined
```
[NOTE] Compute every figure with 02 (`--set` overrides), section 6 for retainer math. Lead with the option you want them to pick; the 01 rubric favors option 1.

### 5. Assumptions

<<Access to people, systems and documents by <date>. One point of contact. Decisions within <n> business days. Work performed remotely except <n> on-site days/mo.>>

### 6. Next steps

Reply to accept an option. I'll send the SOW below for signature, with your MSA or my standard terms. Earliest start: <<DATE>>, after signature and PO.

## PART B - STATEMENT OF WORK

**SOW #** <<n>> under <<MSA dated ____ / the terms below>>
**Client:** <<CLIENT LEGAL ENTITY>>, <<ADDRESS>>
**Contractor:** <<YOUR LLC LEGAL NAME or YOUR NAME>>, <<ADDRESS>>, EIN/TIN on W-9
**Effective date:** <<DATE>> · **Term:** <<DATE>> to <<DATE>>, renewing month-to-month unless either party gives notice

[NOTE] Contract through your LLC if you have one, never as an individual if the LLC exists.

### B1. Background

<<One paragraph: client need, why an external SME.>>

### B2. Scope of services

Contractor will provide the following services ("Services"):
<<10. Service one, verb-first, bounded.>>
<<20. Service two.>>
<<30. On-call SME support up to <n> h/mo by phone, email or video.>>

### B3. Out of scope

Anything not listed in B2, including: <<people management, on-call outside business hours, production operations, legal/regulatory sign-off, ...>>. Out-of-scope work requires a written change order (B8).

### B4. Deliverables and acceptance

```
#   DELIVERABLE        ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA                         REVIEW PERIOD
D1  <<name>>           <<objective test, e.g., covers items a-d>>  <<5>> business days
D2  <<name>>           <<...>>                                     <<5>> business days
```
A deliverable is accepted when the client signs off, or automatically if no written, specific deficiency notice arrives within the review period.

### B5. Schedule

```
MILESTONE              DATE
kickoff                <<date>>
D1 delivered           <<date>>
D2 delivered           <<date>>
end of initial term    <<date>>
```
Dates shift day-for-day with client delays (access, approvals, reviews).

### B6. Client responsibilities

Client provides: a named point of contact; timely access to <<people, systems, documents>>; decisions within <<n>> business days; any equipment or credentials required by client security policy.

### B7. Fees, invoicing, payment

```
ITEM                    TERM
rate                    $<<TARGET>>/h
retainer (if chosen)    $<<amount>>/mo for <<n>> h/mo, billed in advance; unused hours
                        roll over at most <<1>> mo; overage billed at $<<TARGET>>/h in arrears
minimum increment       <<0.5>> h; ad-hoc calls <<1>> h minimum
travel                  time billed at $<<rate>>/h; expenses at cost with receipts,
                        pre-approved above $<<amount>>
invoicing               <<monthly>>, with hours log by task
payment                 net <<30>> d from invoice; late balances accrue <<1>>%/mo
rate review             annually, <<3>>% escalator or re-priced at renewal
```

### B8. Change control

Either party may request a change in writing. Contractor responds with impact on scope, schedule and fees within <<3>> business days. No change is binding until both sign.

### B9. Independent contractor status

Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee. Contractor controls the means, methods, schedule and location of the work (subject to B6 access needs), uses its own equipment except where client security requires otherwise, may serve other clients, and is responsible for its own taxes, insurance and benefits.
[NOTE] This clause must match reality. If the client wants fixed hours, daily supervision or the old job back, see 01 section 50 (agency route).

### B10. Intellectual property

Upon full payment, client owns the deliverables created specifically for client under this SOW. Contractor retains all pre-existing materials, tools, methods and general know-how ("Background IP") and grants client a non-exclusive, perpetual license to Background IP only as embedded in the deliverables.

### B11. Confidentiality

Per the MSA, or: each party protects the other's confidential information with reasonable care for <<3>> yr after the term, with standard exclusions (public, already known, independently developed, legally compelled).

### B12. Term and termination

Either party may terminate on <<30>> d written notice. Client pays for Services performed and expenses incurred through the termination date; prepaid unused retainer hours are <<refunded / credited>>.

### B13. Liability and insurance

Each party's total liability is capped at fees paid under this SOW in the <<6>> mo before the claim; neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages. Contractor carries <<professional liability $____ / general liability $____>>.
[NOTE] Get insurance quotes before agreeing to any limit the client demands.

### B14. Non-solicitation

<<Mutual, <6> mo after term: neither party solicits the other's employees for employment; general job postings excepted.>> [NOTE] Strike entirely if they don't raise it.
