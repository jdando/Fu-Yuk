## WINDSCREEN DESIGN OPTION 1: **TALL EXPANDING TAPER** ⭐ RECOMMENDED

### Concept:
18-20" tall transparent screen with REVERSE taper (expands upward, opposite of current)

### Specifications:
- **Base width**: 10" (254mm) - matches current screen mount points
- **Mid-height width** (at 9"): 11-12" (279-305mm) - gradual expansion
- **Top width**: 14-16" (356-406mm) at 18-20" height - wide enough for shoulders
- **Height**: 18-20" (457-508mm) total from headlight top
- **Rake**: 15° rearward from vertical (your spec)
- **Deflection curve**: 5° at base → 18-20° at top (compound curve)
- **Thickness**: 4mm (5/32") polycarbonate
- **Tint**: Smoke or light tint (maintains visibility, reduces glare)

### Taper Logic:
```
Top (20"):    [────────14-16"────────]  ← Shoulder width protection
              ╱                      ╲
Mid (9"):   [──────11-12"──────]      ← Transition zone
            ╱                  ╲
Base (0"): [─────10"─────]            ← Headlight mount (current)
```

### Why This Works:
1. **Expanding taper** = bubble expands as it rises (correct aerodynamic principle)
2. **Top width 14-16"** at 20" height protects shoulders at Y=±7-8" (16" total)
3. **Compound curve** maintains flow attachment (prevents separation)
4. **4" bubble drop** means if we start protection at chest (Z=9"), it sinks to Z=5" at rider = still covers mid-torso
5. **Transparent throughout** = maintains visibility, Italian aesthetic

### Pros:
✓ Single piece - no complex alignment
✓ Correct aerodynamic taper (expands bubble width)
✓ Meets 18-20" height requirement
✓ 15° rake as specified
✓ See-through maintains bike aesthetic
✓ Protects shoulders (16" wide at top vs 26" needed = good coverage at height)
✓ Cold-formable or thermoformable

### Cons:
✗ Top width (16") still narrower than full shoulder width (26")
✗ Requires compound curve forming (moderate complexity)
✗ Visually larger than current 7"×9" flyscreen

### Expected Performance:
- **Depth gain**: 8/10 (height extends bubble projection)
- **Height protection**: 9/10 (reaches chin at 20")
- **Width at height**: 7/10 (16" covers most of shoulders, not all)
- **Italian aesthetic**: 8/10 (single elegant curved piece)

### Fabrication Approach:

**Thermoforming (Preferred)**:
- 24"×24"×4mm polycarbonate sheet ($35-45)
- Create plywood or MDF form/buck for compound curve
- Local plastics shop can thermoform over buck ($50-100 service)
- Or rent/borrow heat blanket and DIY form at home
- Cut to final profile, hand-polish edges

**Cold Forming (If no thermoforming access)**:
- Multiple shallow curves formed progressively
- Heat lines with heat gun, bend over forms
- More time-consuming but achievable
- Requires patience and test pieces

### Material Specs:
- **Polycarbonate** (not acrylic) - 250× stronger impact resistance
- **4mm (5/32") thickness** - optimal for bike speed vibration
- **Smoke tint or light bronze** - reduces glare, maintains Italian aesthetic
- **UV-coated both sides** - prevents yellowing
