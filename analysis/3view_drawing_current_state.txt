## CRITICAL AERODYNAMIC FINDINGS SUMMARY:

THREE SIMULTANEOUS PROBLEMS:

1. DEPTH PROBLEM (X-axis):
   • Calm zone projects only 13-14" behind screen
   • Rider sits at 18" behind screen
   • Gap: 4-5" shortfall in bubble projection
   • Solution: Extend bubble via height increase + flow energization

2. HEIGHT PROBLEM (Z-axis):
   • Bubble SINKS as it travels rearward
   • Starts at chest height (Z≈5") at screen
   • Drops to lap height (Z≈2") at rider position
   • Sink rate: ~6-8" drop over 18" travel (~20-25° downward)
   • Chin at Z=20" is in pure freestream (no protection)
   • Solution: Taller screen (18-20") to keep protection at shoulder/chin height

3. WIDTH PROBLEM (Y-axis):
   • Screen: 7" wide
   • Bubble at rider: 12-13" wide (1.75x expansion)
   • Shoulders: 26" wide (need 2x more coverage)
   • Gap: 6.5" per side = 13" total shortfall
   • Elbows: 19-23" wide (also exposed)
   • Solution: Lateral deflectors extending ±6-7" from screen edges

FLOW CHARACTERISTICS:
• Vortex shedding from screen edges → shoulder pulsing (turbulent)
• Thick edge (1/4") creates strong vortex formation
• Recirculation zone exists 0-4" behind screen top (calm)
• Shear layer at bubble boundary (13-14" aft) = turbulent transition
• Freestream "very close" to protected pocket edge
• Air "puffing back and downward" at boundary = classic separated flow
